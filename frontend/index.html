🏠 About Us

Made with Love & Tradition

Apus Kitchen started with a simple dream — to serve wholesome, home-cooked meals to busy Mumbaikars who miss the taste of their mother's kitchen. Every dish is prepared fresh each morning using traditional recipes passed down through generations.

We believe great food doesn't need fancy plating or exotic ingredients. It needs fresh produce, the right spices, and a whole lot of love. That's the Apus Kitchen promise.

Our Story

What began as a small kitchen serving neighbours in a Mumbai suburb has grown into a beloved tiffin service trusted by hundreds of families. We still cook the same way — small batches, fresh ingredients, and recipes that remind you of home.

Every meal we prepare carries the warmth of a home kitchen and the reliability of a professional service. Because we know that when you're far from home, a good meal can make all the difference.
